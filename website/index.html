06 · Questions

Owners ask us this.

Do I need to install anything?
No. It runs in the browser. On a phone, add it to the home screen and it opens like an app. See how.
Does it send quotes to clients by itself?
Never. You preview the PDF and choose to share it, download it or open it.
Who can see the profit?
Only the owner. Staff can prepare quotes and enter costs, but reports stay with the owner.
Is my data safe?
Each company’s data is kept separate and backed up daily. You can download it whenever you want.
What happens after the 60 days?
Choose a monthly plan, or stop and take your data with you.
