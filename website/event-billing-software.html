Questions

Owners ask us.

Can I make GST bills for events?

Yes. EventScope adds CGST and SGST, or IGST for clients in another state, and prints your GSTIN on every invoice. You can also bill without GST.

Can the client pay in parts?

Yes. Record the advance and later payments; the bill shows what has been received and the balance due.

Do I need to install software?

No. EventScope runs in the browser and can be added to your phone home screen like an app.
