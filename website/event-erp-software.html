Questions

Owners ask us.

Is EventScope an ERP?

It covers what an event company needs from an ERP — sales, billing, planning, costs, staff and reports — in one simple app, without a long setup.

Can my team use it?

Yes. Staff can prepare quotes, enter costs and fill day sheets. Pay, reports and profit stay with the owner.

Is my data safe?

Each company’s data is kept separate and backed up every day. You can download all of it whenever you want.
