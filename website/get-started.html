Set up in ten minutes

Get started.

Fill in your company once, then put EventScope on your phone.

Step 1

Set up your company.

Open Settings and fill each tab once. Your details then appear on every quote, bill and contract.

1

Company

Your name, phone, address and logo. They sit at the top of every quote, bill and contract.

2

Bank

Where clients pay you. It is printed on your bills.

3

Invoice

Your GST rate, bill numbering and standard terms.

4

Contract

Your standard terms: cancellation and payment rules.

5

Calendar & reminders

The most events you can run in a day, and how early to be reminded.

6

Services

Your price list. Pick from it when you build a quote.

7

Team

Add teammates by their Gmail. Staff handle events. Owners also see reports and profit.

Step 2

Put it on your phone.

No app store needed. Add EventScope to your home screen and it opens full-screen, like any app.

iPhone

Use Safari
1Open app.eventscope.in in Safari.
2Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
3Tap Add. EventScope is now on your home screen.

Android

Use Chrome
1Open app.eventscope.in in Chrome.
2Tap the menu (three dots), then Install app.
3Tap Install. Open EventScope from your home screen.

Sign in with Google the first time. For reminders, open Settings, then Calendar & reminders, and turn them on for that phone. On iPhone, add it to the home screen first.

Then add your first event.
